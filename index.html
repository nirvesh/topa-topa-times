Pink Moment

Valley Turns Out as Autumn Light Returns to the Topa Topas

By the Valley Desk

For a few minutes each evening this week, the whole east wall of the valley caught fire. The famous alpenglow that gives our mountains their reputation arrived early this year, and residents from Meiners Oaks to the East End gathered on porches and pull-outs to watch the rock face flush from gold to deep rose and back to shadow.

Longtime observers say the effect has been unusually vivid, a consequence of clear autumn air and low humidity settling over the range after a dry summer. The display, known locally as the pink moment, lasts only as long as the sun sits at the right angle below the western ridge — rarely more than a quarter of an hour.

Local painters and photographers have staked out their favorite vantage points, while cafes along the arcade report a brisk trade in the golden hour. "People stop mid-sentence," one shopkeeper said. "You learn to just let them look."

Town Hall

Council Weighs Slower Speeds Along the Arcade

By the City Desk

A proposal to lower the limit through the downtown arcade to fifteen miles an hour drew a full room this week, as merchants and cyclists made the case that a walkable center is the town's best asset.

Supporters pointed to weekend foot traffic and the growing number of families arriving by bicycle from the bike path. Opponents worried about delivery access and pass-through timing. The council took no vote, referring the matter to a study session next month, but several members signaled sympathy for a calmer, quieter main street.

A companion measure would add shade trees and two crossings near the post office, funded in part by a county grant that must be claimed before the end of the fiscal year.

Around Town

Farmers Market Adds a Winter Sunday

Beginning next month, the Sunday market will run through the cold season for the first time, with citrus growers and a rotating cast of bakers filling the lot behind the arcade. Organizers cite steady demand and a mild forecast.

Arts

Silent Film Night Returns to the Playhouse

A restored 1925 comedy will screen Friday with live piano accompaniment, part of a season devoted to early cinema. Tickets are by donation, and the lobby will host a small exhibit of vintage lobby cards from the archive.

Outdoors

Trail Crew Reopens the Pratt Route

Volunteers cleared the last washed-out switchback this weekend, restoring the full loop for the first time since spring. Hikers are asked to stay on tread through the regrowth and to pack out what they carry in.
